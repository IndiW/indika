# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Practicing optometrists and residents testing clinical recall during spare moments between patients. Dense domain knowledge, time-pressed, comfortable with medical terminology. They want to feel sharp, not tutored.

## Product Purpose

Daily diagnostic puzzle game where players identify an eye condition from up to six sequential clinical hints. One puzzle per day; share results without spoilers. Exists to sharpen diagnostic recall and give clinicians a reason to talk about cases with colleagues.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, authoritative, precise. Feels like a premium medical journal or a well-designed case log, not a consumer game. Three words: composed, expert, exacting.

## Anti-references

- NHS / government health portals: institutional blue-white, bureaucratic padding, accessibility-first styling that reads as generic
- SaaS dashboards (Linear, Notion): sidebar nav, density-as-design, utility aesthetic
- Wordle and Wordle clones: pastel tiles, playful emoji, consumer-game casual

## Design Principles

1. The case speaks for itself. Clinical hints are the product; UI chrome should disappear.
2. Confidence without decoration. Premium feel comes from precision: tight type, considered spacing, nothing gratuitous.
3. One task, one screen. No competing affordances. The only action in play is submitting a diagnosis.
4. Authority through typography. Serif title signals a journal masthead; geometric body keeps clinical text legible.
5. Color earns meaning. Teal = correct and active. Coral = dismissed hypothesis. Everything else is neutral.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on dark background. No color-only information encoding (always pair color with shape/text).
